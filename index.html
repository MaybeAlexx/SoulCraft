About SoulCraft

Soulcraft, founded by Hmwis and Pengo in 2023, started as a server just for friends and has since grown through six unique seasons. Now, we’re turning the page to a new chapter—a fresh beginning for our community.

We’re a kind, reasonable, and mature group of players who value creativity, fun, and good company. Whether you’re here to survive, build, or simply hang out, Soulcraft is a welcoming place to be.
